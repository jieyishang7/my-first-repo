3D Studies

Two Three.js scenes exploring light, depth, and spatial composition in 3D.

Cyberpunk City Blocks

A navigable neon city composed of towers, illuminated windows, holographic signs, intersecting streets, traffic light trails, star particles, and floating hearts.

Statement. I used this exercise to construct an urban atmosphere from a limited set of Three.js primitives, asking how repeated boxes, planes, lights, and grids could read as a dense cyberpunk city. The scene combines fog, emissive windows, holographic signs, moving traffic signals, pulsing hearts, star particles, subtle building movement, and continuous rotation to create depth and activity without an external dataset. Orbit controls let viewers rotate, pan, and zoom through the model, shifting the work from a fixed image into an explorable city block.

Atmospheric Geometry

An orbital field of UCLA and Columbia identity panels surrounding a neon energy core, animated rings, particles, and floating hearts.

Statement. I attempted to turn the institutional identities of UCLA and Columbia into spatial material rather than presenting them as flat graphic marks. Procedurally drawn school-name textures orbit a pulsing energy core with metallic panels, neon rings, particle light, and floating hearts, using each institution's recognizable colors and typographic character as the artistic reference. The panels continuously face the camera, while orbit controls allow viewers to rotate, pan, and zoom through the composition; no external dataset is used.
